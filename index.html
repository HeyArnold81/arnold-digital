Questions

The things you're probably wondering.

Can't see your question? Get in touch and ask me directly.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need. I price projects around the actual scope rather than adding unnecessary features. You'll know the agreed cost before work starts.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the required content and feedback are available. I'll give you a realistic timeframe before the project starts.

Do I need to provide all the content?

Not necessarily. I'll need your knowledge of the business, but I can help structure and improve the website copy so visitors can quickly understand what you offer.

Can you help with domains and hosting?

Yes. I can help you understand the options and get the website set up correctly without making the process more complicated than it needs to be.

Will my website work properly on phones?

Yes. Mobile isn't treated as an afterthought. The website will be designed to work across modern phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Can you improve my existing website?

Potentially, yes. I'll first look at what you already have and whether improving it makes more sense than rebuilding it.

Do I own my website?

Ownership and any third-party services used will be made clear before the project starts. There should be no surprises about what you're paying for or what happens if you want to move elsewhere later.

Can you help after the website goes live?

Yes. If you want ongoing help with changes, fixes or small improvements, we can agree an appropriate support arrangement.

I already use Instagram or Facebook. Do I really need a website?

Social media can be an excellent way to promote a business, but a website gives you a professional home online that you control and gives customers another reliable way to find and contact you.

Can you help with things other than websites?

Yes. If you have a repetitive process, awkward form, disconnected tools or another practical technology problem, tell me about it. If it's something I can sensibly help with, I'll explain the options.

Will my website appear on Google?

I'll build the website with sound technical SEO foundations and make sure it can be crawled properly. Search rankings depend on many factors, though, so nobody can honestly guarantee a particular position.

Will my website be secure?

Security is considered throughout the build. I favour simple architectures, HTTPS, minimal dependencies and sensible security controls rather than adding unnecessary technology that creates more things to maintain.
