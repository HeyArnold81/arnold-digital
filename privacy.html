Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Arnold Digital may collect through this website, why it is needed and how it is handled.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Who is responsible for your information?

Arnold Digital is operated by Phil.

Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@arnolddigital.co.uk .

What information may be collected?

If you contact Arnold Digital through the website enquiry form, the information collected may include:

  • Your name
  • Your business name, if provided
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number, if provided
  • The type of help you are interested in
  • The information you include in your message

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not relevant to your enquiry.

Why is this information used?

Information submitted through the website is intended to be used only where reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand what service or assistance you are requesting
  • Discuss a potential project or piece of work
  • Provide requested quotations or information
  • Maintain appropriate business correspondence

Legal basis

Depending on the nature of the enquiry, personal information may be processed because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, or because Arnold Digital has a legitimate interest in responding to genuine business enquiries.

How long is information kept?

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry and manage any resulting business relationship. Information may be retained for longer where reasonably required for business, accounting or legal purposes.

Who receives your information?

Arnold Digital does not intend to sell personal information.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Website enquiries are protected and processed using Cloudflare services, including Turnstile and Workers, before being delivered by email. Google Gmail is used to receive business email.

These service providers may process limited personal or technical information where necessary to provide their services.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use advertising trackers or non-essential analytics.

Cloudflare Turnstile is used on the enquiry form to help distinguish genuine visitors from automated traffic. It may process technical information necessary to provide that security service.

If non-essential analytics or other tracking technologies are introduced in future, the privacy and consent requirements will be reviewed before they are enabled.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be used to protect information handled through this website. The site is designed around a minimal static architecture, HTTPS and limited third-party code.

No website or online service can honestly guarantee absolute security.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights in relation to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing.

You may also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, services or technology used by Arnold Digital changes.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
